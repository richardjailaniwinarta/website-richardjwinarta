Lesson ReVAmp with Richard

An invite page for SGB VA students and prospective leads to join a live Portfolio Audit & Discovery Call mock-up session I host, so they can sharpen their portfolio before talking to real clients.

GoalFill a live session
AudienceSGB VA students & leads
FormatPortfolio audit + mock call
What I built
  • Personal hero — my photo and a clear promise: "Refine, Audit, and Elevate".
  • Live countdown — days, hours, minutes and seconds to the registration deadline for urgency.
  • 3-part session breakdown — Portfolio Audit, Discovery Call Mock Up, Feedback & Next Step.
  • Single CTA — one bold "Register Now" button, nothing to distract.
  • Auto-close state — after the deadline the page switches to "Registration is closed".

GoalCapture email leads
OfferFree 7-day meal plan
Built withWix Studio
What I built
  • Benefit-first headline — "Lose weight without the struggle" with one clear free offer.
