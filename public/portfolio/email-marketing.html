Campaign strategy · Ocean Smith (coaching)

A full campaign, from hello to booked call

For a coaching business, I developed a complete email campaign strategy: each email has one job, one clear call to action and a subject line built to be opened. Together they take a new subscriber from "who is this?" to booking a discovery call.

For you: a ready-to-send sequence written in your voice, with subject line options for every email, so you can launch without staring at a blank page.

The sequence
  • 1 · Welcome email — builds trust, introduces the coach and the offer, and invites a first reply.
  • 2 · Value-driven email — a quick mindset exercise plus a free worksheet that proves expertise.
  • 3 · Promotional email — a clear offer with limited discovery-call slots and one booking link.

How I build your campaign

Strategy first, then copy that converts

Every campaign starts with your goal and your reader, not a template. I map the journey, write each email around one clear action, design it to look on-brand in the inbox, and set it up to send automatically, so your list keeps warming up leads while you work.

Why it works: I've been in sales since 2015, so I write emails the way I'd talk to a buyer: clear, honest and focused on the next step. Clients notice: short, straight-to-the-point emails people actually read.
